How it works

Three steps to a stable Discord

01

Diagnostics

The app checks available ws-nodes in real time and measures the response of every tunnel.

02

Node selection

Out of all nodes it picks the fastest WebSocket server with the minimal latency.

03

Connection

Discord switches to the chosen tunnel: keep-alive and auto-reconnect keep the channel open, the DNS cache is flushed.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How is Zapret Discord different from a VPN?

Only Discord traffic is proxied — everything else goes directly. That makes it faster, lighter on the system and it needs no administrator rights.

What is ws transport?

Discord talks to the proxy over WebSocket — the same protocol ordinary websites use. The channel is hard to tell apart from web traffic, so it works where direct connections break.

Is there anything to configure manually?

No. The app checks the ws-nodes itself, picks the fastest tunnel and connects with a single button.

Is it free?

Yes. Version 1.0.0 is completely free — no ads, no subscriptions, no traffic limits.

What data do you collect?

None. The servers keep no request logs, and all settings are stored only on your computer.
